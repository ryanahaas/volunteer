About Me

I am a youth worker, community organizer, graduate student and writer with twelve years of experience working and volunteering in schools, universities, afterschool programs, and community organizations.

I volunteer because I believe more caring cities get built in small, ordinary ways — by showing up, being useful, and letting a community shape me as much as I hope to contribute to it. I initiate volunteer relationships by listening, learning how a program and community works, and contributing to what's already underway. I move at the speed of trust, letting relationships, capacity, and mutual understanding shape what I offer and when.
