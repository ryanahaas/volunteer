Why I volunteer

I believe more caring cities get built in small, ordinary ways: by showing up, being useful, and letting a community shape me as much as I hope to contribute to it. When I join a program, I start by listening and learning how things already work. I like to move at the speed of trust, letting relationships and the program's own needs shape what I offer, when, and how.
