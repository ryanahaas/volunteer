About Me

I am a youth worker and educator with twelve years of experience working and volunteering in afterschool programs, elementary schools, universities, and community organizations. Much of that time has been with K-6 kids: tutoring, homework help, facilitating afterschool programming, and helping new sites get up and running.

Currently a graduate student, I volunteer because I believe more caring cities get built in small, ordinary ways — by showing up, being useful, and letting a community shape me as much as I hope to contribute to it. I start by listening and learning how a program already works, contributing to what's already underway and aligning with a community's established norms. I like to move at the speed of trust, letting relationships, capacity, and mutual understanding shape what I can offer, when, and how.
